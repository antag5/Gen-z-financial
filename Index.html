What You Should Know About Credit Cards

A credit card lets you borrow money from a bank and pay it back later. Unlike a debit card, it does not take money directly from your bank account.

How To Gain Credit Score

  • Pay bills on time
  • Keep balances low
  • Use credit responsibly
  • Check your credit report

How To Use Credit Cards

Spend only what you can afford to pay back and always make payments on time.

Common Credit Card Mistakes

  • Paying only the minimum balance
  • Missing payments
  • Maxing out cards
  • Overspending

Safety Tips & Fraud Prevention

  • Never share your PIN
  • Use secure websites
  • Check statements regularly
  • Report suspicious activity
